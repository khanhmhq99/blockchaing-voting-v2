## Retained requirements and decisions

Preserve public MVP 1/2/2.5 contracts and election history. A new versioned election would issue a client-generated anonymous credential, freeze one public membership root, derive a deployment/election-specific nullifier scope, encrypt an exact-one ballot on the client under a fresh independent election key, and atomically check eligibility and ballot validity on chain. The accepted ciphertext, key/manifest binding and proof evidence must be publicly reconstructible. Normal tally decrypts aggregates only and checks decryption evidence; the single key holder can still decrypt archived individual ciphertexts. The authenticated issuance channel and unauthenticated ballot channel require separate storage, credentials and ingress before claiming an operator trust boundary. No permanent voter wallet is required.

Approach A, with a reviewed ballot circuit, is the preferred **research direction** because each public statement can be specified and checked separately. The final DAVINCI patch exposes record fields as public signals **and closes the padded-field underconstraint**. The research contract reconstructs these signals from the actual record and frozen election state. Valid local acceptance, ciphertext recording and mutation rejection were observed, including 2,948-byte full calldata and 1,053,309 gas. Two accepted records were internally aggregated and decrypted to `[1,1]`; no public decryption proof exists. Edge headless ballot proving took 5,559.60 ms in one run. This selects a **local feasibility composition**, not a deployment-ready cryptographic stack. Approach B remains a contingency if the independent circuit cannot be reviewed. DAVINCI is not approved as the production ballot component.

## Blocking findings

1. **Contract-only adaptation is insufficient.** The original three-signal circuit proof hides the record behind `inputs_hash`. Its exact Poseidon(16) tree has no installed reviewed Solidity verifier. The minimum circuit change makes all record fields public; a new R1CS, WASM, setup key, vkey and Solidity verifier are mandatory. The local research probe succeeded with that change, while the original wrapper remains unsafe.
2. The circuit commits to process ID, ballot mode, encryption key, DAVINCI address, vote ID, all 16 ciphertexts and weight. It does not directly include candidate-order manifest, chain ID or contract. The research contract freezes a canonical 128-byte manifest digest and derives process ID/scope from chain, deployment and manifest. The **upstream circuit had a padded-field underconstraint**: it accepted nonzero inactive plaintext and unchecked padded ciphertext. The final patch forces inactive plaintext zero and validates all 16 ciphertexts; only active fields enter tally. [Exact research encoding](BALLOT_SPECIFICATION.md) and cross-language review remain.
3. The setup provenance remains unresolved despite a technically valid final beacon key. On 2026-10-14 the owner contribution `0001`, returned contribution `0002`, and precommitted drand beacon `#3` all passed `snarkjs zkey verify` against the pinned R1CS and ptau. The second contributor supplied only the file; no independently communicated hashes or statement about private entropy control were received. A successful transcript check alone cannot establish contributor independence. Full phase-one transcript verification, DAVINCI source license and combined GPL distribution terms, current audits and review of this composition remain unresolved. The final-key local contract/browser probes passed, but they do not remove those blockers. [Ceremony evidence](TRUSTED_SETUP.md).
4. Two actual accepted records were reconciled against successful transaction calldata, aggregated with BabyJubJub, and internally decrypted to `[1,1]`. A reviewed, publicly checkable single-holder aggregate-decryption proof and production accepted-set finality/reorg protocol were **not** demonstrated. See [tally verification](TALLY_VERIFICATION.md).
5. One Edge headless browser ballot proof passed, but mobile memory bounds, browser encryption/Semaphore end-to-end, and BSC testnet EVM/precompile compatibility remain unmeasured. The local Hardhat 31337 result cannot stand in for BSC. Concurrent nullifier races and production issuer/ballot separation remain untested.
6. A focused 2026-10-14 [source and circuit review](SOURCE_AND_CIRCUIT_REVIEW.md) found no explicit DAVINCI grant at the pinned commit. Distribution of patched source and generated artifacts cannot be assumed lawful from GitHub visibility or an AGPL license in another repository. A draft rightsholder request and independent cryptographic-review request are ready but unsent. The local `.sym`/vkey check confirmed all 72 signals; focused padding and reversed-manifest probes passed. `circom --inspect` warned about a standalone `MaskGenerator` assertion not represented in R1CS. No bypass was demonstrated with the contract's fixed `numFields=2`; this finding and all helper constraints still need independent review. There is no basis yet to revise the circuit or invalidate the existing ceremony artifacts, and there is no basis to approve their deployment.

Neither research-only wrapper may accept real ballots. Do not start Checkpoints 1–7 or deploy either wrapper. The immediate ceremony step is to obtain the second contributor's independently calculated hashes and explicit entropy-control attestation, complete phase-one transcript validation, and independently review the circuit/source and license. Final-artifact complete-record and one desktop browser probe already passed; a compatible public aggregate-decryption proof, mobile bounds and BSC compatibility remain required. No anonymity, independent tally verifiability, coercion resistance, receipt-freeness, protection from compromised clients or honest credential issuance is established by this local probe.
