The archive contains seven ZKeys: the four listed above plus QV message processor, QV tally and `MessageProcessorNonQv`. It contains **no R1CS, WASM, vkey, manifest, checksum file or phase-two transcript**. The vkeys above were exported locally with `snarkjs@0.7.6`; their SHA-256 values identify those exports, not an independently published registry. `MessageProcessorFull` is the candidate for one-credit `FULL` voting; `VoteTallyNonQv` is the corresponding tally path. The checked-in `circom/circuits.json` declares **test** circuits with depth 10 and different batch parameters; they must not be matched to the production keys. [Upstream trusted-setup documentation](https://maci.pse.dev/docs/security/trusted-setup) claims the v3 small ceremony completed and gives maxima but does not itself supply exact downloaded-file hashes or phase-two transcript verification. Source/package license review is [separate](LICENSE_AND_VERSION_REVIEW.md).

The archive members were streamed and hashed with `python research/mvp3/maci/inventory-archive.py <archive>`; extracted keys and exported vkeys were hashed by `node research/mvp3/maci/inventory-artifacts.mjs <directory>`. Both tools label files `UNVERIFIED`. Next obtain exact production R1CS/WASM, independent published hashes and ceremony transcripts. Verify R1CS/ZKey relationship, phase-one/two transcript to available evidence, exported vkey versus on-chain registry/verifier, real proof off chain and on chain, and malformed proof rejection. A successful proof cannot establish an honest ceremony. Until then these are **officially published, provenance-incomplete artifacts (category B)**, not production-trusted parameters.
