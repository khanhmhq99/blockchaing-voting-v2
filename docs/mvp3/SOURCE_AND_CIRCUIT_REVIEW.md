## Source and artifact rights

Neither root nor `js/package.json` declares a `license` field. A search of the pinned README and circuits found no SPDX or explicit grant. A public npm registry query for `@vocdoni/davinci-circom` returned 404, so no published package metadata supplied a separate grant. Other Vocdoni repositories, including [davinci-contracts](https://github.com/vocdoni/davinci-contracts), publish AGPL terms; that **does not establish terms for this repository**. The DAVINCI paper's description of the system as open source is not a grant with identifiable conditions for this commit. [GitHub's no-license guidance](https://choosealicense.com/no-permission/) explains why public source visibility alone does not supply general modification or redistribution rights.

Accordingly, the project cannot currently assert permission to redistribute the modified DAVINCI circuit source, patch, compiled R1CS/WASM, proving key, generated verification key, or derivative verifier. Local technical evaluation does not resolve redistribution or public/testnet artifact publication. Do not publish or send further DAVINCI-derived materials while this is unresolved. ## Focused cryptographic review

| Claim and adversary | Inspection and observed check | Status and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A submitter cannot substitute another public ballot statement under one valid proof. | `audit-public-signals.mjs` checked the first 72 `.sym` entries against the contract's `[mode,address,weight,process,voteId,key,ciphertext,inputsHash]` order. The final vkey has `nPublic=72` and 73 IC points. `BallotProof` constrains `inputs_hasher.out === inputs_hash`; its `MultiPoseidon(71)` hashes process, mode, key, address, vote ID, all 16 four-coordinate ciphertexts and weight. | Focused source/order check passed. The contract builds signals 0–70 from frozen state and the actual submitted ciphertext and sets 71 to Semaphore's message. Independent review of Circom/R1CS semantics and the off-chain builder is still needed. |
| A submitter cannot smuggle an invalid or choice-bearing value into padding. | The patch enforces `fields[i] * (1-mask[i]) === 0` and `fieldComparator[i].equal === 1` for **all 16** fields. The final-key Hardhat probe rejected nonzero padded plaintext during proving and changed padded ciphertext at acceptance. | Focused positive/negative checks passed. Only fields 0 and 1 enter the tally, so independent review must confirm that excluding authenticated zero encryptions from tally is correct for the intended aggregation. |
| A submitter cannot swap the election public key or use an invalid key in a valid proof. | Public signals 5–6 come from contract immutables; `BabyCheckPrimeSubgroup` checks on-curve and subgroup order, and the circuit rejects identity. Final-key probe rejected a valid proof made for a different key. | Cryptographic key binding passed locally. The constructor only range-checks coordinates; election setup must separately prove key generation, private-key possession and fresh independent election key. Client encryption randomness remains a client-security assumption. |
| A submitter cannot move a proof between candidate orders, elections or deployments. | The constructor requires a canonical 128-byte ABI manifest, stores its Keccak-256 digest, and derives canonical-field `processId` and `electionScope` from chain ID, contract address and digest. The circuit commits to process ID; Semaphore commits to the same message under scope. Final-key probe rejected changed manifest, swapped candidate order, changed scope and cross-deployment replay. | Binding relies on Keccak-256 collision resistance, correct chain ID and immutable deployment state. Candidate IDs and manifest are **indirectly** bound; they are not direct Poseidon inputs. Independent cross-language encoding review is still needed. |
| The circuit enforces its stated two-candidate mode. | Contract fixes packed mode, address `0`, weight `1`; `CheckBallotMode` bounds active fields and sum. Missing, multiple and out-of-range choices were rejected in final-key witness probes. `circom2 --inspect` exited 0 but warned that `MaskGenerator.control.out` is absent from the parent constraints because source uses `assert(control.out == 1)`. | The standalone assertion is not an R1CS constraint. In this composition `numFields=2` is a checked public signal, and `BallotCipher.valid_fields === num_fields` supplies a separate bound: `valid_fields` is the sum of at most 16 products of Boolean `equal` and Boolean `mask`, hence at most 16; the unpacker confines `num_fields` to 8 bits, so no field wrap can equate a value above 16 to that sum. No accepted-statement exploit from this warning was demonstrated. An independent reviewer must confirm this reasoning, especially before generic mode reuse. The other inspect warnings in circomlib templates also require review rather than automatic dismissal. |

`circom2@0.2.23 --inspect` exited 0 with warnings, not a clean audit. The warnings include unused `MaskGenerator.control.out`, some `LessThan`, `EscalarMul` and `VoteIDChecker` intermediate signals. [Circom's assertion documentation](https://docs.circom.io/circom-language/code-quality/code-assertion/) distinguishes witness-time `assert` from an R1CS `===` constraint; its [inspect documentation](https://docs.circom.io/circom-language/code-quality/inspect/) says unused-signal warnings merit review. The exact command was `node node_modules/circom2/cli.js circuits/ballot_proof.circom --inspect -l circuits -l node_modules` from the pinned clone. `snarkjs@0.7.6 r1cs info` exited 0 with 139,744 constraints and 72 public inputs. `node research/mvp3/audit-public-signals.mjs` exited 0. The final-key Hardhat probe exited 0 after the added padding/candidate-order mutations; it observed 2,948-byte calldata, first/second vote gas 1,053,297/1,053,261, `wrongPaddedCiphertext=true` and `wrongCandidateOrder=true`. These are local research observations, not proof of circuit soundness.

## Independent review deliverables and change rule

Give an independent reviewer the pinned source revision, patch, R1CS/WASM hashes, final vkey/verifier hashes, [ballot specification](BALLOT_SPECIFICATION.md), this finding list and the exact positive/negative probes. Ask for a written report that names assumptions, adversaries, severity, reproducible findings and a signed-off public-input map. In particular, review the `MaskGenerator` warning, 72-signal ordering, active/padded field equations, BabyJubJub subgroup and nonidentity checks, `k` handling, exact `MultiPoseidon` tree, indirect manifest/process/scope binding and accepted-event/public-audit reconstruction. Do not give any private election key, witness, entropy, identity join or secret configuration to the reviewer.

If the reviewer finds a soundness, privacy or required-mode defect, fix source and rerun circuit compilation, R1CS/WASM hashes, phase-two setup from `0000`, independent contribution, precommitted beacon, vkey/Solidity verifier export, browser and full local mutation probes. The current `0002` and final zkey **cannot be transferred to a changed R1CS**. If only contract encoding changes, recompile/revalidate the contract and determine whether circuit artifacts still match. Keep MVP 3 deployment and Checkpoint 1 blocked until rights, independent review and the other cryptographic gates pass.
